About the App

SmartArt Solutions lets you take a picture of any artwork and instantly tells you what it is, providing descriptions and context using AI and museum databases. Perfect for museum visits, art students, or anyone curious about art.

Features

  • Identify artworks instantly using your camera
  • Get detailed descriptions and historical context
  • Save your favorite artworks
  • Access curated museum data
  • Subscription-based premium content for art enthusiasts
